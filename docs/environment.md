

# Camera and room: what the player needs

Tell your players this before the first session (the runtime's health advice says the same during play). Each limit carries how well it is supported by evidence: `empirical` decided a measured outcome; `live-supported` behaved as stated in live scans; `provisional` was crossed live without a measured outcome; `unknown` was never observed beyond the limit.

| What | Limit | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Camera frame rate | at least 24 frames a second | unknown |
| Light on the player | picture brightness at least 40 of 255 | unknown |
| Distance (hand size in the picture) | a palm between 7 % and 40 % of the picture's height | provisional |

Also: one player in the picture; the camera at about chest to head height, facing the player; the whole of what the game asks for in view (the capability catalogue gives each one's framing). The runtime holds the camera permission: your game never asks for the camera.
